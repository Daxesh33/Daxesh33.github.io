ArrowOut Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

ArrowOut ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Daxesh33 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, hints, levels in progress) and settings (board theme, sound and vibration) are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: an optional video ad for an extra hint or heart, and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a completed level. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (or an app-specific ID if the advertising ID is unavailable),
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example which ads were shown and tapped) and diagnostics.

Google processes this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for teens (maximum rating "PG"), and ad requests are not marked as directed to children.

Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from Theme → Privacy options in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you can always keep playing without watching one (for example by retrying a level).

Analytics and crash reports

The current version of the app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services. If a future version adds them, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the information collected will be limited to anonymous gameplay and technical data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for optional touch feedback (can be turned off in the app).
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help to remove it.

Security and retention

Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app and do not keep any information about you. Retention of advertising data is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
